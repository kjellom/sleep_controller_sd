Current budget (12V rail): fans ×2 at 75% ~380mA · TDA2050 ×2 moderate volume ~800mA · buck converters ~330mA · total ~1.5A typical, ~2.3A worst case. 12V 5A supply recommended for overnight use.
RC soft-start recommended for permanent build: 10Ω series resistor + 1000µF capacitor to GND on buck converter 5V output before ESP32 VIN. Slows power ramp to ~10ms, prevents boot instability from fast PSU rise time.

Lessons learned

IO13 — strapping pin. Never use for driven outputs. Caused repeated SD_MMC boot failures. Permanently retired.

IO12 — leave completely unconnected. Caused SD_MMC failure when wired.

PCM5102 strap pins — all four must be tied. Floating FMT causes loud noise. Floating XSMT causes silence or noise.

Gate resistor color codes — 100Ω (brown-black-brown) vs 100kΩ (brown-black-yellow). Verify with multimeter before installing.

Star ground — buck input and output GND on separate wires. USB shield must tie to common ground.

SD card slot — spring-loaded slot is fussy on SunFounder board. Card ejection caused multiple apparent failures.

WAV header — use findWAVDataOffset() parser rather than fixed 44-byte offset.

IO22 — exclusively I2C SCL. Do not share with any other peripheral.

5V audio rail — PCM5102 on dedicated buck converter 5V rail reduces noise and ESP32 regulator load.
